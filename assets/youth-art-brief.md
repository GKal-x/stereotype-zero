# Teen and student atlas v1

Built-in image generation in generate mode, using memories-v1.png as style and protagonist identity reference. The output is preserved unchanged in youth-v1.png. Eight measured bounds in youth-atlas.js map the school-age adolescent and young adult to four directions. Existing pixel-grid leg rigging supplies movement. These are provisional age and costume interpretations, not exact period likenesses. The older adult and childhood atlases are retained unchanged.

## Final prompt

Exactly EIGHT full-body sprites in TWO ROWS, FOUR COLUMNS. Columns in each row: front/down, left profile, right profile, back/up. Row 1: the dark brown tousled-haired protagonist as a 16-year-old adolescent, visibly longer torso and legs than the child in reference; no facial hair. Cream T-shirt with an open muted rust overshirt, dark blue jeans, cream trainers. Row 2: same protagonist as a 20-year-old university student, slightly more mature proportions, brown tousled hair with youthful full hairline, very light short stubble, navy blue T-shirt with teal pocket, blue jeans and cream trainers. Preserve compact readable JRPG style, friendly restrained expressions, crisp dark outlines and simple pixel-friendly colour clusters. Whole bodies including both feet, consistent clothes and identity in every direction. No child proportions, no full adult beard, no aging/receding hairline, no scout uniform, no glossy or painterly filter, no blur. True transparent alpha outside sprites, generous equal cells, no ground shadow, no background, no text or labels.
