## Education mobilisation

Contemporary [June 2006 reporting](https://www.in.gr/2006/06/13/greece/anaballetai-i-katathesi-toy-nomosxedioy-gia-tin-anwtati-ekpaideysi/) describes the postponement of the framework-law bill, protests and further planned demonstrations. A school pupil participating in wider education mobilisation is a different claim from the law directly governing that school building. The assembly gameplay separates these issues. No invented vote is attributed to the actual school.

## December 2008 and policing

[Amnesty’s 11 December 2008 statement](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2008/12/greek-police-use-punitive-violence-against-peaceful-demonstrators-20081211/) reports on police violence against demonstrators following the killing of Alexandros Grigoropoulos. Its [2009 report on policing demonstrations](https://www.amnesty.org/ru/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/eur250012009en.pdf) concerns December 2008 and January 2009 allegations. These document broader context; they do not establish this narrator’s detention or identify a particular station or officer.

The user explicitly clarified that they remained at the same school building and were not at the Exarcheia shooting. The news scene therefore begins in Plaka. Being dragged to a station and hit as children is retained as personal testimony. Names, exact date, station and official dialogue are not supplied. An empty bench, a choice to record rather than dwell, and aftermath interactions convey the recollection. There is no combat reward for enduring abuse and no claim that the child could have prevented it with a correct move.

## TEI and political chronology

[Parliament’s Law 4009/2011 record](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/Nomothetiko-Ergo/Psifisthenta-Nomoschedia/?law_id=e571250c-f0ee-4b88-a566-0ed17f4b05a5) places publication at Gazette 195 A, 6 September 2011. [Contemporary reporting](https://www.tovima.gr/2011/08/31/society/diamantopoyloy-kata-prytanewn-gia-tis-katalipseis/) records opposition and occupations at universities and TEI. The campus is an authored Psachna adaptation of the mechanical-engineering studies the user described.

The [Prime Minister’s historical biography of Samaras](https://www.primeminister.gr/prime-minister/antonis-samaras) establishes the coalition formed after June 2012, supported by ND, PASOK and Democratic Left, with the latter’s support ending in June 2013. This must not be collapsed into the passage of the 2011 law under the preceding political context.

The user dates the independent-student challenge against DAP to approximately 2012–13 and describes anger at prioritising party and government over study needs. The specific win is not yet identified as an election, assembly vote or campaign outcome. One [local report of the 2012 TEI election](https://www.egnomi.gr/article/26948/thriambos_tis_dap_stis_ekloges_sto_tei_xalkidas.html) describes a DAP victory; it therefore cannot substantiate an independent electoral win and may concern a different contest. Gameplay preserves the personal memory and stages composite political discussion, with no manufactured tally or alleged quotation from a real student.
