# Public historical chronology for the prologue

Research pass: 13 October 2026. This document contains public history only. It does not establish anyone's private biography or attendance at an event. It is an initial sourced chronology, not a completed investigation into every school, neighbourhood or year.

## 2004 Athens Olympics

The Summer Olympic Games in Athens took place 13–29 August 2004. This is a calendar anchor, not evidence that every resident experienced prosperity, optimism or participation. Personal reactions and specific locations require separate recollections and period evidence.

- [Australian Olympic Committee: Athens 2004](https://www.olympics.com.au/games/athens-2004/) — official Olympic committee dates and overview. Its wider narrative is an institutional account, not a survey of residents.
- [IOC Olympic World Library: Athens 2004 marketing overview](https://library.olympics.com/digitalCollection/DigitalCollectionAttachmentDownloadHandler.ashx?documentId=355189&parentDocumentId=355188&skipCopyright=true&skipWatermark=true) — contemporary planned event dates. Many figures are projections; do not use them as verified final totals.

## 2004–2009 government

The relevant prime minister is Kostas (Konstantinos A.) Karamanlis, who took office in March 2004, was returned after the 2007 election and served until the 2009 election. Distinguish him from the older Konstantinos Karamanlis, his uncle. The official biography is useful for dates but presents an institutional/political account of achievements. Its claim about his parliamentary status is historical page text and must not be treated as current office information.

- [Prime Minister's office: Kostas Karamanlis](https://www.primeminister.gr/prime-minister/kostas-karamanlis).
- [Government Secretariat: first Karamanlis cabinet, March 2004–September 2007](https://gslegal.gov.gr/government/karamanli-a-konstantinou-3/).
- [Hellenic Parliament: election results](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/Vouli-ton-Ellinon/To-Politevma/Ekloges/Eklogika-apotelesmata-New) — includes the election of 4 October 2009.

## 6 December 2008 and the demonstrations

Fifteen-year-old Alexandros (Alexis) Grigoropoulos was killed by a police officer on 6 December 2008 in Exarcheia, Athens. Demonstrations and unrest followed in Athens and elsewhere. Student participation, peaceful protest, police abuse allegations and destructive clashes must be distinguished rather than collapsed into one generic riot scene.

Amnesty International's 11 December 2008 account records reports of disproportionate force and ill-treatment of peaceful demonstrators, including its own members. It is contemporary human-rights reporting and attributes allegations and eyewitness accounts; it is not the later court judgment. Details of the shooting disputed in that early report should not be presented as its final legal finding.

- [Amnesty International, 11 December 2008](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2008/12/greek-police-use-punitive-violence-against-peaceful-demonstrators-20081211/) — event date, age and contemporary policing reports.
- [Amnesty International, March 2009: Alleged abuses in the policing of demonstrations](https://www.amnesty.org/ru/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/eur250012009en.pdf) — period-specific investigation, with discussion of students and wider social grievances. The report's March 2009 date matters; a recent search-engine publication date for the hosted PDF is not the event date.
- [Reuters photograph/caption, 13 December 2008](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/school-friends-of-alexandros-grigoropoulos-sit-during-a-silence-protest-in-front-of-greek-parliament-building-in-athens/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMDg6bmV3c21sX0dNMUU0Q0QxSU9BMDI) — records a silent protest by school friends, useful evidence of forms of participation beyond clashes. Reference only; no image licence acquired.
- [Associated Press, 6 December 2024](https://apnews.com/article/6180bdb61c1fb37a03fdf74be0c4c42c) — retrospective identification of the killing and later proceedings. This does not justify depicting 2024 policing, equipment or transport closures in a 2008 scene.

Design implication: distinguish historical material the character learns through conversation/news from events personally experienced. Exact protest routes, school groups, dates, clothing, street appearance and police equipment need further period research once a scene is selected. A remembered feeling of insecurity belongs to the character's viewpoint; it is not a universal claim about every Greek resident.

## 2009–2010 economic crisis

Use a sequence rather than a single abrupt switch: the 2008–2009 global crisis and worsening Greek fiscal situation, the Greek debt crisis's escalation, then the first financial assistance programme in 2010. The Eurogroup announced the first programme on 2 May 2010. Public events cannot establish when an individual household experienced a loss of income.

- [Council of the EU: Greek financial assistance programmes](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/financial-assistance-eurozone-members/greece-programme/) — first programme announced 2 May 2010, with later programmes distinguished. Archived institutional account.
- [Council of the EU: financial assistance background](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/financial-assistance-eurozone-members/) — euro-area crisis in relation to the preceding global financial crisis.
- [European Stability Mechanism: Lessons from financial assistance to Greece](https://www.esm.europa.eu/system/files/document/2021-07/lessons-financial-assistance-greece.pdf) — search-indexed extract identifies October 2009 deficit revisions and escalation by spring 2010. Full PDF retrieval returned 403 in this pass: do not represent it as fully reviewed.
- [European Commission assessment of EU fiscal rules, 2019](https://commission.europa.eu/system/files/2019-09/2019-09-10-assessment-of-eu-fiscal-rules_en.pdf) — indexed material on 2009 fiscal reporting and the period preceding the first programme. Not a source for a single-cause explanation of the crisis.

Do not claim the Olympics alone caused the debt crisis. School stress, family conflict and economic insecurity can overlap without being reduced to one cause or having dates inferred from one another.

## Psachna / Chalkida higher education

Institution names and departments changed over time. Contemporary identity matters: a scene before the 2013 merger should not casually use a later institutional name, and an earlier college experience should not be described through the present NKUA campus as though it were unchanged.

- [Nuffic: Greek institutions and programmes](https://www.nuffic.nl/onderwijssystemen/griekenland/onderwijsinstellingen-en-opleidingen) — education-system reference records the 2013 merger of TEI Chalkida and TEI Lamia into TEI of Central Greece and its 2013–2019 period. The entry is a reference for institutional history, not a judgment of educational quality.
- [NKUA laboratory's institutional account](https://renewablelaben.wordpress.com/about/) — describes the Evripos complex in Psachna and its establishment under Law 4589/2019 following integration of former TEI departments. Laboratory-authored page; cross-check department-specific continuity against official records before scene production.
- [NKUA Digital Industry Technologies: campus contact/access](https://en.dind.uoa.gr/contact_and_access) — present-day Psachna location; not a source for historical bus schedules or the narrator's department.
- [External institutional evaluation report, TEI of Central Greece](https://teiunion.gr/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/619-final_external_evaluation_report_tei_of_central_greece.pdf) — potentially useful evaluation document; full retrieval timed out in this pass. Not yet reviewed and cannot substantiate claims about historical campus conditions.
- [Contemporary statement by Business Administration students/staff, 6 February 2013, republished by Alfavita](https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/anakoinoseis/91254_tei-halkidas-enantia-sto-shedio-athina) — primary participant statement hosted by an education outlet about a specific proposed reorganisation. Do not assume it concerns the narrator's course, year or participation.

The exact entry year and department are required to select the correct name, curriculum and relevant institutional changes. A personal description of an unhappy or limited experience is not evidence that the whole institution was objectively bad.
