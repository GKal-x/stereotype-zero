# Stereotype Zero — Athens research dossier

Research date: 12 October 2026. Version 0.1: initial desk research and scenario research framework.

This is a foundation, not a completed ethnography or final scenario. No interviews or on-site observation have been conducted. Some academic pages were accessible only through indexed extracts; their full text needs review before detailed claims are used. Current district-level drug markets, crime rates, rents, bus connections and service eligibility have NOT been established by this pass.

## 1. Creative brief preserved from the discussion

Android exploration RPG with Pokémon-inspired district progression and encounters, darker contemporary storytelling, and an original identity. A financially insecure male protagonist lives in Kato Patisia. Themes include housing, work, transport, capitalism, bureaucracy, police, politics, environmental conditions, drugs, family, siblings, friendship, university, ambitions, sexuality and relationships. Relationships must extend across the ensemble, rather than exist only around the player.

Geography: Petralona, Thissio, Monastiraki, Omonia, Victoria, Attiki, Agios Nikolaos, Kato Patisia, Agios Eleftherios, Ano Patisia, the Patision corridor, Kypseli/Plateia Amerikis/Koliatsou branches, Exarcheia, Nea Filadelfeia and separate Zefyri chapter. Perissos, Pefkakia and Nea Ionia are useful northern connections. Expansion farther along Line 1 is possible, not committed scope.

Working title: Stereotype Zero. Earlier supernatural battle ideas and a redevelopment conspiracy remain optional proposals, not approved requirements.

## 2. Evidence rules

Every future claim should carry: source, publication date, observation/data year, geographical scope, method, confidence, disagreements and intended creative use.

- Established evidence: a directly supported finding at its actual scale.
- Interpretation: the researcher's explanation, attributed to its author.
- Reputation: a perception whose speaker and context matter.
- Fiction: invented people, businesses, dialogue, events and plot.
- Unresolved: a plausible proposition needing investigation.

National statistics cannot establish conditions on one Athens street. Sale-price indices are not rent indices. Arrests are not a representative measure of offending. Wastewater measurements do not locate individual users or prove which neighborhood contains a market. Announcement dates are not necessarily opening dates. A recent publication may use older data.

## 3. Findings with immediate scenario relevance

### Housing and ownership

Athens Social Atlas explains how post-war antiparochi construction shaped apartment life and how living conditions and social positions can differ between lower and upper floors. Its foundational vertical-segregation article is from 2015 and uses 2011 census evidence; it supports a historical structure, not a complete 2026 district profile. [S1]

Scenario inference: the home building can contain several class positions. Tenure, inherited property, floor, repairs and family support may matter as much as the district name. A lift repair or shared heating dispute can connect otherwise separate households.

A 2026 Atlas entry identifies vacant housing as a complex field involving ownership arrangements, inactive properties and methodological difficulties. It should be examined alongside affordability research; empty does not automatically mean habitable or available. Only an indexed extract was reviewed here. [S2]

The Bank of Greece reports Athens apartment prices increasing 5.9% year-on-year in Q2 2025. This is a dated sale-price finding, not a statement about present neighborhood rents. Its data portal is the appropriate route for subsequent releases. [S3]

Scenario inference: distinguish an owner with little cash, a tenant with stable wages, a person with inherited housing, and a worker without a financial buffer. Their vulnerabilities differ.

### Gentrification and tourism

Alexandri's 2015 Atlas article examines displacement and changing uses in central Athens, including Petralona, while warning that theories developed elsewhere need local interpretation. It is historical groundwork. A 2026 research article by Gourzis and Alexandri examines changing growth models and links among regeneration, tourism and housing financialisation. The latter was located through its abstract and requires full review. [S4–S5]

Scenario inference: investigate who benefits, who pays and who can stay after a change. A renovated building, better business income and displacement can coexist. Avoid assigning every housing problem to one cause without evidence.

### Family, independence and intimate life

Eurostat's September 2026 publication reports an estimated home-leaving age of 30.9 in Greece for 2025, compared with 26.3 across the EU. Its measure is derived from household co-residence among ages 15–34, not a direct survey of each person's first move or proof of any single cause. This is national context. [S6]

Scenario inference: investigate privacy, parental support, financial dependence, caring responsibilities and unequal sibling opportunities. A character living with parents may have money but little autonomy; another may have independence but no safety net. Do not infer dating habits or family attitudes from the statistic.

The December 2025 Atlas entry on the family apartment building offers a promising source for kinship and housing. Only its indexed summary was reviewed; its detailed conclusions are not yet established here. [S7]

### Drugs, addiction and services

EKTEPN is the national monitoring source. Its harm-reduction page explicitly says it belongs to the 2025 report, includes developments to early July 2025, and mostly uses 2023 analytical data. It describes outreach, low-threshold services, food, hygiene, housing support and referral, and records the Athens supervised consumption facility STEKI 46 starting in April 2022. The site also lists newer reports for follow-up. [S8–S9]

Scenario inference: separate recreational use, dependence, street homelessness, dealing, organized exploitation, treatment and recovery. Include alcohol and gambling in the research scope. A service worker, user, relative and nearby shopkeeper may have different accounts of the same place.

No street-level drug-market map has been verified. Omonia, Victoria, Exarcheia and Zefyri must not receive automatic drug prevalence or criminality rankings based on reputation. Police releases and testimony can contribute, but need corroboration and dates.

### Politics and policing

Amnesty's June 2026 report concerns patterns of unlawful force and impunity in policing protests in Greece. It is a rights organization's investigation, which should be attributed and read alongside documented official responses and case outcomes. It is not evidence that every police encounter follows one pattern. [S10]

An official police notice dated 8 October 2026 describes Exarcheia metro-construction traffic restrictions from 10 October 2026. This establishes a specific contemporary construction context, not a station opening date. [S11]

Scenario inference: construction can alter familiar walks, deliveries and meeting places. Political disagreement can involve access, process, housing and public space. Investigate the arguments of affected residents and project authorities rather than inventing consensus.

### Heat and unequal comfort

An Athens municipal announcement from summer 2025 describes a heat response including services and advice for residents. It establishes an example of public intervention, not guaranteed provision for every later heatwave. [S12]

Scenario inference: investigate shade, water, cooling costs, outdoor work and care for older relatives. Heat should change routes and schedules, with consequences differentiated by housing and resources.

## 6. Transport research

STASY confirms the core sequence: Petralona → Thissio → Monastiraki → Omonia → Victoria → Attiki → Agios Nikolaos → Kato Patisia → Agios Eleftherios → Ano Patisia → Perissos → Pefkakia → Nea Ionia. [S16]

OASA Telematics supplies bus/trolley line information. [S17] No specific route number to Zefyri or Nea Filadelfeia is approved by this dossier. Verify actual endpoints, stops, operating patterns and walking links. Train time does not include the whole door-to-door journey.

For each selected connection record: route number/name, direction, boarding stop, destination stop, transfer, walking access, timetable date, fare basis, accessibility and a gameplay compression decision. Preserve meaningful connectivity rather than every real street.

## 7. Everyday life and ensemble research

Research work in retail, delivery, hospitality, offices, trades and informal arrangements separately. Assemble plausible monthly budgets for a tenant, someone living with family, a student-worker, a precarious worker and a low-income owner. Do not set game prices until wages, hours, household size, rent, bills and dates are aligned.

Research university enrollment and campus geography, timetable demands, commuting, exams, meals, housing support and work conflicts using the relevant institutions. Do not assume the historic Polytechnic campus contains every contemporary course.

For relationships investigate everyday meeting places, privacy, dating expenses, digital communication, gender expectations, queer experiences, care work and migration decisions. Statistics establish constraints; interviews and qualitative research help explain experience. No relationship customs have yet been established by this pass.

Each eventual character dossier needs: livelihood, tenure, schedule, family resources, desire, fear, boundaries, political outlook, contradictions and relationships with at least two other cast members. Cast members need independent events and decisions.

## 8. Fieldwork and source triangulation

Suggested research activities, not activities already completed:

- Compare weekday morning, afternoon, evening and weekend routines on selected routes.
- Record public-space features, businesses, shade, traffic, accessibility and sound without identifying vulnerable individuals.
- Seek consent for interviews and quotations. No outreach or messages have been sent.
- Include tenants, owners, shop workers, students, parents, older people, migrants, outreach workers and disabled travelers.
- Ask what has changed, what outsiders misunderstand, what costs time or money, and what makes staying worthwhile.
- Treat testimony as situated experience; compare accounts and corroborate consequential factual claims.
- Use crime reporting to investigate incidents, not to assign moral identities to neighborhoods.

3. Verified transit connections and service locations for the chosen setting month.
4. Household budgets and work/student schedules.

Completion criterion: each major conflict has a documented material context, multiple situated perspectives and a plausible consequence. ## 12. Additional findings: work, education, migration and inequality

### Improving aggregate employment can coexist with difficult lives

The OECD's 2026 report *Challenges for Young People in Greece to Reach Financial Independence* connects education, employment, housing and family life. Its summary reports improving youth employment since 2013 while identifying continuing problems with wages, seasonal work and skills mismatch. These are national findings; the report's age bands must be preserved when extracting figures. [S19]

Scenario inference: contemporary Athens should not simply repeat peak financial-crisis conditions. A character may find work more readily yet still struggle to achieve stability. The scenario should investigate job quality, hours, predictability and housing costs alongside unemployment.

### University has material infrastructure

AUEB describes a refectory in its main building and free-meal access for qualifying students through a meal card. Its English information page was last updated in September 2020, so current rules require confirmation. The Student Club's indexed site contains 2026 operational announcements. A Ministry of Education notice dated 29 July 2026 concerns the 2025–26 student housing allowance application deadline. These establish concrete research routes, not eligibility for the protagonist. [S20–S22]

Scenario inference: the university chapter can involve meals, documentation, deadlines, commuting and study groups. A free lunch can also be where friendships form. Aid should have institution-specific requirements rather than function as a generic reward.

### Migration includes commerce and agency

The Atlas case study *Immigrant entrepreneurship in central Athens* documents differentiated business geographies, including the Victoria area, and connects them to successive migration periods. The indexed extract supports further investigation of shops and commercial networks; it does not establish today's ownership of particular premises. [S23]

Scenario inference: migrant characters can be employers, workers, customers, parents, classmates, property owners and organizers. Develop actual commercial and family interests alongside experiences of exclusion.

### Zefyri and national Roma evidence

FRA's 2024 Roma and Traveller survey includes Greece and examines housing, education, employment, health, discrimination and services. The overview describes household sampling and self-identification. Its evidence is useful context, but is not a Zefyri survey. Extract Greek results with methodological notes before using numerical claims. [S24]

Scenario inference: schooling, service access, discrimination and work deserve as much investigation as reported crime. District-specific resident accounts and municipal evidence remain essential.

### Gender and intimate life need their own research

EIGE's Gender Equality Index and care-related data provide source pathways for work, money, time and unpaid care. The methodology changed in 2025, so comparisons need checking. No score or district conclusion is used here. [S25]

Scenario inference: siblings or partners may have unequal time because one performs more unpaid care. Treat this as a character-specific circumstance, not a universal role for women or men. Research legal changes separately from everyday acceptance. Dating, desire, sexual identity, religious outlook and relationship breakdown still need qualitative evidence.

## 13. Provisional story questions generated by the evidence

These questions are creative synthesis, not research findings or a final plot:

1. What does independence mean when the affordable room belongs to your family?
2. What changes when a friend has an inherited flat and you pay rent from similar wages?
3. Can you finish a degree while protecting your income and caring for someone?
4. Who can benefit from an improving neighborhood without being priced out?
5. How do you maintain intimacy when money, privacy and schedules are scarce?
6. What happens when a person's recovery needs conflict with family exhaustion or neighbors' concerns?
7. How does a familiar route change when construction, heat or shifts make it difficult?
8. What can friendship or collective action solve, and where does it meet institutional limits?

The research currently favors intersecting household and neighborhood stories over a single explanation for the whole city. That is an interpretation to test as the evidence grows.
