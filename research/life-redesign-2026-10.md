## Source boundaries

The shooting cut uses the established event: Alexandros Grigoropoulos, fifteen, was killed by a police officer on 6 December 2008 in Exarcheia. It does not reconstruct the disputed detailed sequence, quote participants, or put the narrator at the shooting. Staging and silhouettes are original adaptations.

Contemporary Amnesty report, 11 December 2008, records police violence allegations against peaceful demonstrators and extensive damage: https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2008/12/greek-police-use-punitive-violence-against-peaceful-demonstrators-20081211/

The centre street is a composite, not a claim that a particular named business burned. The station assault and parental hitting derive from the user’s narrated memories. No station, officer, exact detention date, dialogue or detailed parental incident is invented as verified history. Visual blocking is adaptation, explicitly labelled in the scene.

The original camp score remains original; naming Tsopana Rave is not a claim to include their recording or melody.
